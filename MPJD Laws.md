# Moudly Potato Justice Department (*MPJD*)

## General Act | 1 |

**1.1** The Mouldy Potato Justice Department (*MPJD*) is the department of the Mouldy Potato's Discord and Minecraft servers responsible for enforcing the law and administering justice in the Mouldy Potato Discord and Minecraft Server.

**1.2** The Department of Justice carries out functions:
- **1.2.1** Providing and following legal norms.
- **1.2.2** Refinement and reworking of Mouldy Potato Discord and Minecraft server laws and other regulatory documents in cooperation with The Mouldy Potato Supreme Court (*MPSC*), after the laws and others are being initiated and outlined by the Juridical-Law conference. 
- **1.2.3** Prosecute and investigate.
- **1.2.4** Providing internal security and internal investigations by the Server Attorney and the prosecutor's office.
- **1.2.5** Oversight of the Mouldy Potato branch structures that are the affiliates of the Mouldy Potato Justice Department (*MPJD*).
- **1.2.6** Representation of the Mouldy Potato interests in courts.
- **1.2.7** Cooperating with the Mouldy Potato Local Court (*MPLC*) and Mouldy Potato Supreme Court (*MPSC*) in the fight against crime.
- **1.2.8** Providing public access to information about legal processes, court decisions, and the activities of the Department of Justice.
- - **1.2.8.1** If the (MPJD) does not provide information and clarity regarding legal processes and other matters, the Mouldy Potato Supreme Court must send a legal request directly to the Server Attorney for departmental clarification with The Mouldy Potato Prosecutor. If the Server Attorney fails to act, the MPSC may initiate legal proceedings against The Mouldy Potato Prosecutor and possibly against The Server Attorney, demanding transparency and public disclosure of the processes and documents of the MPJD. Following this, the Mouldy Potato Supreme Court may 'veto' all (MPJD) decisions and actions made without proper documentation and clarity.

**1.3** The Department of Justice is headed by the Server Attorney, and partly by the Mouldy Potato Prosecutor. The Server Attorney is the head of the server law enforcement, ensuring that laws are uniformly and adequately enforced and, if necessary, requesting revisions to laws or practices.
- **1.3.1** The Mouldy Potato Prosecutor is the head of the Prosecuting branch of the Mouldy Potato Justice Department.
- - **1.3.1.1** The Mouldy Potato Prosecutor is appointed and can be dismissed by the Law-Juridical Conference. In some extraordinary cases, he can be appointed by the Mouldy Potato Server Attorney.

**1.4**  The legal basis for the activity of the Mouldy Potato Justice Department shall be the General Laws of The Mouldy Potato Discord and Minecraft server and the other normative legal acts and laws of the Mouldy Potato server.

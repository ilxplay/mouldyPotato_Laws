# Moudly Potato Justice Department (*MPJD*)

## General Act | 1 |

**1.1** Department of Justice is the department of the Mouldy Potato's Discord and Minecraft servers responsible for enforcing this law and administering justice in the Mouldy Potato Discord and minecraft Server.

**1.2** The Department of Justice carries out functions:
- **1.2.1** Providing and following legal norms.
- **1.2.2** Preparation and reworking of Mouldy Potato Discord and Minecraft server laws and other regulatory documents in cooperation with The Mouldy Potato Supreme Court (*MPSC*).
- **1.2.3** Prosecute and investigate.
- **1.2.4** Providing internal security and internal investigations by the Server Attorney and the prosecutor's office.
- **1.2.5** Oversight of the Mouldy Potato branch structures that are the affiliates of the Mouldy Potato Justice Department (*MPJD*).
- **1.2.6** Representation of the Mouldy Potato interests in courts.
- **1.2.7** Cooperating with the Mouldy Potato Local Court (*MPLC*) in the fight against crime.
- **1.2.8** Providing public access to information about legal processes, court decisions, and the activities of the Department of Justice.

**1.3** The Department of Justice is headed by the Server Attorney, and partly by the Mouldy Potato Prosecutor. The Server Attorney is the head of the server law enforcement, ensuring that laws are uniformly and adequately enforced and, if necessary, requesting revisions to laws or practices.
- **1.3.1** The Mouldy Potato Prosecutor is the head of the Prosecuting branch of the Mouldy Potato Justice Department.
- - **1.3.1.1** The Mouldy Potato Server Prosecutor is appointed and can be dismissed by the Judicial Conference. In some extraordinary cases, he can be appointed by the Mouldy Potato Server Attorney.
